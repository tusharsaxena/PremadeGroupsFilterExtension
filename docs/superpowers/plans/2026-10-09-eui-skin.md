**Goal:** Paint the attached panel (`modules/Panel.lua`) in the user's EllesmereUI theme, the way
`PremadeGroupsFilter_EllesmereUI` paints PGF's own dialog, through EllesmereUI's public skinning API
(`EllesmereUI.RegisterSkin`, `SKINNING_API.md`, facade `S`, apiVersion 3).

## Owner decisions (2026-10-09)

1. **Gate — every one of these must hold, and each is shown as a status line in the settings page:**
   - **EllesmereUI enabled:** the `EllesmereUI` global with `RegisterSkin`, and its window-skin child
     `EllesmereUIBlizzardSkin` loaded (the child holds the dispatcher; without it `RegisterSkin`
     only queues).
   - **Third-party skinning on:** EllesmereUI's master switch (`EllesmereUIDB.thirdPartySkinsOff` not
     true) **and** this addon's own entry in its Third-Party Addons list
     (`EllesmereUIDB.thirdPartySkinAddons["PremadeGroupsFilterExtension"]` not false). Nil = on.
   - **PGF's own skin active:** `PremadeGroupsFilter_EllesmereUI` loaded and its entry
     (`thirdPartySkinAddons["PremadeGroupsFilter"]`) not false, so PGF's window and this panel
     always match.
2. **Our own switch:** a profile setting (default on). Effective skin = all gate conditions AND the
   switch. When any condition fails the switch is shown disabled and cannot turn the skin on (never
   forced).
3. Built into this addon (not a separate skin addon). `## OptionalDeps: EllesmereUI` so it loads
   first.

## Known constraints

- EllesmereUI dispatches a skin once per session (PLAYER_LOGIN, or at registration if later), and
  only while its switches are on. Skins are not removable live: turning the skin ON mid-session can
  apply at once (keep `S`); turning it OFF needs `/reload` (prompt for it).
- Read EllesmereUI's saved variables read-only, at call time, nil-guarded, from one file (an
  `EUIBridge` in the style of `core/PGFBridge.lua`). Never write them.
- Skin our own frames only. Primitives are taint-safe (alpha-only art removal, no Hide/SetParent).
- The panel's own geometry (header-only collapse built from clipped metal copies, `ATTACH_RAISE`,
  min/max art) must still look right when the metal border is replaced by EllesmereUI's flat shell.
