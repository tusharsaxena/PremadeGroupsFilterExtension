# 02 — Deviations: Ka0s Premade Groups Filter Extension

Audited against **Ka0s WoW Addon Standard v2.77.0 (2026-10-07)**, at commit `58156ca`. Prefix
**`PGE`**, assigned on this first audit. Evidence ids (`E<n>`) point into `03_EVIDENCE.md`.

## Tally, with its basis

Grades are **impact**, not rule strength (`AUDIT.md` step 5). Dependents (`derived from <ID>`) are
excluded from the headline and from the MUST count. The recorded deviation PGE-R01 is accepted and in
neither count.

The 14 root MUST failures are PGE-01, -02, -03, -04, -05, -06, -09, -10, -11, -13, -14, -15, -17, -18.

dependent.

**Verdict: minor deviations.** Nothing a user can hit today as an error or data loss; one Medium where
a filter can silently stop filtering on a client without the deprecated spec globals; the rest is
structure, tests and documentation. The disabled state itself — the check that failed eleven of eleven
addons at the 2026-09-16 sweep — is **compliant** here.
