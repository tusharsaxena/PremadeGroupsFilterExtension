## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

The addon draws one window of its own, the filter panel (`modules/Panel.lua`,
`PortraitFrameTemplateMinimizable` under PGF's dialog, or alone in test mode). Its title band carries
one control, the min/max toggle: the collapse button (chevron up) while expanded and the expand
button (chevron down) while collapsed. The template's close button stays hidden, as before.

- **Tooltips.** Each button gets *Collapse* or *Expand* (`L.PANEL_COLLAPSE_TIP`, `L.PANEL_EXPAND_TIP`)
  through `LibKa0s-Tooltip-1.0`'s `AttachTitleTip(btn, words, addonName)`: one line in the owner's
  `gtip.title`, placed by `PlaceAttached` above the button, flipping below without room, hidden on
  leave, and nothing while stood down. Without the library, the same line above by a relative point.
- **Colors.** Each chevron rests in `icon.tint` (was `icon.arrow`) and takes `text.link` under the
  pointer; the template's highlight stays. The degraded install keeps the art's white, as before.
- **Surface used.** `AttachTitleTip` and the facade's colors (`NS.SkinColor`). `W.HeaderButtons` was
  not adopted: the toggle is PGF's own MaximizeMinimize wiring, which the panel mirrors, and moving it
  onto the library's header marks is the larger change (it is the standalone-windows header-strip
  adoption this bundle already lists as owed).
- **Not a control.** The click band over the rest of the title (`f.headerClick`) is the title itself
  and takes no tooltip: a tooltip over the whole band would open on every pass of the pointer.

Cases: `tests/test_titlebar.lua` (new, six cases): a non-empty tooltip on every control in every
skin, owned by it at `ANCHOR_NONE` in `gtip.title` and hidden on leave; the tooltip above; rest
`icon.tint` and hover `text.link` in every skin; Ka0s Default's and Obsidian's two colors told apart;
none while stood down; the degraded install's tooltip. The first three were red before the change.
`tests/test_panel_layout.lua`'s chevron case re-pins the rest color from `icon.arrow` to `icon.tint`,
by ruling 4. Smoke: `docs/smoke-tests.md` SKIN-55.
